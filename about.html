About Me

Engineer, builder, and lifelong tinkerer.

I am a chemical engineering graduate who enjoys understanding how systems work, improving them, and building practical solutions when an off-the-shelf answer is not enough.

Background

Technical curiosity across disciplines.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering from The University of Texas at San Antonio with a minor in Chemistry. My engineering interests extend beyond traditional process work into mechanical systems, electrical troubleshooting, controls, CAD, fabrication, and prototyping.

That range reflects how I like to work: understand the underlying engineering, get hands-on with the hardware, test the idea, and iterate until the result is practical and reliable.

Outside of formal engineering work, I enjoy building and repairing things, 3D printing, automotive projects, guitar, and exploring new technical tools.
